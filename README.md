
# CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship
## Task 2: Phishing Awareness Training


##  Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Cyber Security Internship at CodeAlpha**.

The objective of this task was to develop a phishing awareness training presentation that educates individuals about phishing attacks, social engineering techniques, suspicious communication patterns, and cybersecurity best practices.

The presentation focuses on helping users recognize phishing attempts, understand their potential impact, and respond appropriately to suspicious messages.

##  Objectives

- Understand the fundamentals of phishing attacks.
- Identify common phishing delivery channels.
- Recognize social engineering techniques used by attackers.
- Understand phishing indicators in emails, SMS, and social media.
- Learn how phishing attacks affect individuals and organizations.
- Explore preventive measures against phishing.
- Understand appropriate phishing incident reporting and response procedures.
- Promote cybersecurity awareness and responsible online behavior.

##  Topics Covered

### 1. Introduction to Phishing
- Definition and purpose of phishing.
- Credential theft and sensitive information exposure.
- Malware distribution through deceptive communications.
- Phishing threats against individuals and organizations.

### 2. Common Phishing Delivery Channels
- Email phishing
- SMS phishing (Smishing)
- Social media phishing
- Voice phishing (Vishing)

### 3. Social Engineering Techniques
- Urgency and fear
- Authority impersonation
- Curiosity-based manipulation
- Personalized deceptive messages

### 4. Phishing Targets
- Finance and HR departments
- Customers and online service users
- Executives and privileged users
- General internet users

### 5. Warning Signs of Phishing
- Suspicious sender addresses or domains
- Poor spelling and grammar
- Unexpected links or attachments
- Requests for sensitive information
- Mismatched URLs and misleading link text

### 6. Impact of Phishing Attacks

**Organizational Impact**
- Data breaches
- Financial losses
- Reputational damage
- Regulatory consequences

**Individual Impact**
- Identity theft
- Financial fraud
- Account compromise
- Personal information exposure

### 7. Prevention and Protection
- Using strong and unique passwords.
- Enabling Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Verifying suspicious requests through official channels.
- Keeping operating systems and security tools updated.
- Avoiding unexpected links and attachments.
- Participating in regular security awareness training.

### 8. Phishing Incident Response
- Avoid interacting with suspicious messages.
- Report phishing to the appropriate security team.
- Change compromised passwords immediately.
- Scan affected devices for malware.
- Notify affected contacts when necessary.
- Preserve evidence for investigation.

### 9. Phishing Reporting and Evidence Collection
- Forward original phishing emails to designated security contacts.
- Report suspicious SMS and social media messages through platform reporting mechanisms.
- Preserve sender details, timestamps, screenshots, and relevant interaction information.
- Use appropriate external reporting channels where applicable.

##  Key Learning Outcomes

Through this task, I developed a better understanding of:

- **Phishing Identification:** Recognizing suspicious emails, messages, URLs, and attachments.
- **Social Engineering Awareness:** Understanding how attackers manipulate human emotions and trust.
- **Threat Awareness:** Learning how phishing can lead to credential theft, malware infection, and unauthorized access.
- **Security Awareness:** Understanding the importance of user education in organizational cybersecurity.
- **Incident Response Fundamentals:** Learning the initial steps to take when phishing is suspected or confirmed.
- **Evidence Preservation:** Understanding the importance of collecting relevant information before reporting an incident.
- **Security Best Practices:** Applying MFA, password hygiene, sender verification, and secure communication practices.
- **Defensive Security Mindset:** Developing a proactive approach to identifying and preventing security threats.
